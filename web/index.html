1. Pick a design
Want to design your own?

Any AI chat app (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and the like) can draw a design that fits this booth exactly.

  1. Copy the instructions below.
  2. Paste them into your AI app and write your idea at the end. It gives you one PNG picture. Save it to your phone (Photos or Files, either works).
  3. Come back here, tap Add my design and pick it. The booth shows where the photos will go; name it and add it. It stays on this booth, updates included.

This WiFi has no internet. Copy first, switch to your usual WiFi for the AI app, then rejoin this one to add the files.
