WHAT TO PRODUCE
Two files with the same name, for example party1.png and party1.json.
1. PNG: the artwork, RGBA, fully transparent wherever a photo shows through.
2. JSON: where the photos go, and the design's name:
   {"name": "Party Night", "slots": [[x, y, width, height], ...]}
Draw it with code (Python + Pillow works well) so the sizes are exact. Image
generators cannot hit exact pixel sizes or true transparency. If you can run code,
run it and give me both files to download. If not, give me one script that writes both.

HARD RULES (all three layouts; the booth checks every one)
- The PNG is exactly the size for its layout, has an alpha channel (RGBA) and is
  under 20 MB.
- 1 to 8 slots. Each is [x, y, width, height] in whole pixels from the top left,
  at least 20 x 20, and fully inside the canvas.
- List slots in the order the photos are taken (first photo first).
- Every pixel inside a slot is fully transparent (alpha 0). A little decoration over
  a slot's edge is fine (tape, a sticker), but at least half of every slot must be
  transparent or the booth turns the design away.
- The JSON is valid and has the same file name as the PNG.

HOW THE BOOTH USES IT
- Each photo is 16:9 landscape. It is center-cropped to fill its slot, then the PNG
  is laid on top. Wide slots keep most of the photo. Tall slots, common on portrait
  sheets, keep only the middle (a 460 x 600 slot keeps under half the width).
- The photo extends 5 px past the slot on every side, so no gap shows at the edge.
- Anything opaque drawn over a slot (tape, a sticker, a frame edge) covers the photo.
- The area behind the PNG is white, so transparent pixels outside the slots print white.
- If the date stamp is on, it sits in the bottom right corner of each photo. The
  digits are about 7.5% of the slot height, inset about 8% of the slot height from
  the bottom and 6% of the slot's longer side from the right. Keep that corner
  clear, or add "stamp": "bl", "tl" or "tr" to the JSON to move it to another corner.
- "name" in the JSON (up to 60 characters) is what the booth calls the design.

DESIGN TIPS
- Keep text and important art at least 60 px from every edge. The printer trims a little.
- Make text at least 40 px tall with strong contrast. A strip is only 2 inches wide.
- Leave at least 20 px of frame between slots.
- A thin border or mat around each slot makes the photos look finished.
- Sheets have room for a big title, names and artwork. Strips do not: keep strip
  text short.
